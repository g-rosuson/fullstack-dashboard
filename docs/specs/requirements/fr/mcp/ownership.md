# Ownership

Who may act on a prompt or a conversation.

- **FR-MCP-OWN-001** — Only the user who started a prompt shall receive its progress, respond to its permission ask, or stop it.
- **FR-MCP-OWN-002** — A permission response or a stop for another user’s prompt shall be indistinguishable from a missing prompt.
- **FR-MCP-OWN-003** — Only the user who created a conversation shall read it, delete it, or start a prompt in it.
- **FR-MCP-OWN-004** — A read, delete, or prompt for another user’s conversation shall be indistinguishable from a missing conversation.
